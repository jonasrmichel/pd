# Persona roster

Use this index to pick personas for a panel. Each persona's full definition lives in
`../<skill-name>/SKILL.md`, which is the single source of truth.

| Skill name | Persona | Core question | Invoke when… |
|---|---|---|---|
| `competitor-ceo` | Competitor CEO | Why should I worry? | Idea feels like a winner; need differentiation, a moat, or prep for tough questions |
| `simplifier-storyteller` | Simplifier & Storyteller | What could you remove and still keep the magic? | Too complicated; feature creep; pitch prep; inconsistent narrative |
| `pragmatic-scientist` | Pragmatic Scientist | Can this actually happen? | Exciting but unrealistic; entering execution; need risks, assumptions, resources |
| `tech-navigator` | Tech Navigator | What is really causing the problem? | Stuck troubleshooting; unclear if user/process/system issue; workarounds piling up |
| `consumer-champion` | Consumer Champion | Why would your audience care about your idea? | Unvalidated demand; assumptions about user needs; "would anyone use this?" |
| `engineering-challenger` | Engineering Challenger | How can we solve the hardest problem? | Technically hard; "can't be done"; feasibility; perf/reliability/cost trade-offs |
| `growth-partner` | Growth Partner | Where is the value (marketing & commercial lens)? | Commercial opportunity; who pays; value prop; how it scales |
| `visionary-optimist` | Visionary Optimist | What could this become? | Incremental or safe; stuck in constraints; need ambition or energy |

## Natural tensions
Include at least one of these pairs in every auto-selected panel, because the useful insight is
usually in the disagreement.

- Visionary Optimist ↔ Pragmatic Scientist (ambition vs. realism)
- Visionary Optimist ↔ Simplifier & Storyteller (expand vs. cut)
- Consumer Champion ↔ Growth Partner (user value vs. commercial value)
- Competitor CEO ↔ Growth Partner (threat vs. opportunity)
- Tech Navigator ↔ Engineering Challenger (route around vs. solve head-on)

## Default panels by artifact
| Reviewing… | Suggested personas |
|---|---|
| New product or feature idea | Consumer Champion, Growth Partner, Competitor CEO, Pragmatic Scientist |
| PRD or spec | Simplifier & Storyteller, Consumer Champion, Pragmatic Scientist, Engineering Challenger |
| Pitch, deck, or narrative | Simplifier & Storyteller, Competitor CEO, Growth Partner, Visionary Optimist |
| Roadmap or strategy | Visionary Optimist, Pragmatic Scientist, Competitor CEO, Growth Partner |
| Technical design or architecture | Engineering Challenger, Tech Navigator, Pragmatic Scientist, Simplifier & Storyteller |
| Stuck project or recurring problem | Tech Navigator, Engineering Challenger, Consumer Champion |
